# Closed-Test Tester Recruitment

Assets for recruiting the closed-testing cohort for Perpenda on Google Play.

**Goal:** ~12 *trustable* testers (real product people), kept continuously active
for the 14-day window, so we can graduate to production access.

**The one rule that keeps the cohort clean:** never broadcast a public opt-in
link. In closed testing *you* hold the guest list — testers join by the Google
email you add. Invite named people you know, or vetted referrals. If you post in
a community, say "DM me" and screen in the DM; only then add their email.

Over-recruit: aim for ~18–20 to keep 12 continuously active.

---

## 1. The 1:1 invite (for people you know)

> **Subject:** Mind testing something I built? (PM thing, ~2 weeks)
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> I built an app called **Perpenda** — it helps product people get *decision-grade*
> on LLMs: enough fluency to make build / buy / skip calls and hold your own with
> engineers. Not a math course, not a hype feed — just the trade-offs that actually
> come up.
>
> It's in Google Play closed testing and I'm pulling together a small group of ~12
> product people **I actually trust** to pressure-test it for two weeks. You came
> to mind because [specific reason — *you've shipped AI features / your product
> judgment is sharp / I'd genuinely value your eye on it*].
>
> The ask is light: install it (Android), run through a couple of units, and tell
> me where it feels off — especially if the **grading** ever feels wrong. Maybe
> 10–15 min total across the two weeks.
>
> If you're in, reply with the **Google email on your Android phone** and I'll add
> you + send a 2-minute join guide. Totally fine to say no — no hard feelings.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]

Don't skip the specific-reason line — it's the part that converts.

## 2. Warm-referral version (when someone is introduced to you)

> Hey [Name] — [Mutual] mentioned you'd be a good person to put Perpenda in front
> of. It's a tool to help PMs get decision-grade on LLMs, currently in a small
> closed test (~2 weeks, ~10–15 min total). I'm keeping the group small and
> hand-picked. If you're curious, send me the Google email on your Android phone
> and I'll add you. No worries if not.

---

## 3. "How to join" guide (send after they say yes)

> **Joining the Perpenda test — 2 minutes**
>
> 1. Make sure you've sent me the **Google account email on your Android phone**.
>    I'll add you — access usually lands within a few hours.
> 2. On your phone, open this link: **[opt-in link from Play Console]**
> 3. Tap **Become a tester** / accept.
> 4. Tap through to Google Play → **Install**. *(If it says "not available," wait
>    an hour and refresh — Play lags after you're added.)*
> 5. Open the app, create an account, start **Unit 1**.
>
> That's it — ping me if anything's weird. 🙏

Grab the opt-in link from Play Console → *Testing → Closed testing → your track →
Testers tab*.

---

## 4. LinkedIn post (for when your direct network is tapped out)

No public link. The point is to surface people, then screen them in DMs.

> I've spent the last while building **Perpenda** — and I'm looking for a small
> group of product people to help me pressure-test it.
>
> The premise: PMs, founders, and design leads keep getting handed AI decisions
> they don't feel fully equipped to make. Build it or buy it? Is this eval good
> enough to ship? Why is the model confidently wrong? Perpenda is built to get
> you **decision-grade** on exactly those calls — trade-off-first, with grading
> that's honest about its own confidence. Not a math course. Not a hype feed.
>
> It's in Google Play closed testing and I want ~12 senior product people to put
> it through its paces for two weeks. Light lift (~10–15 min total), real
> feedback, especially on where it feels off.
>
> If that's you — **drop a comment or DM me** and I'll get you in. (Android, for
> now.)

When someone responds: ask one screening question — *"What do you work on?"* — and
only add testers who fit the audience. That single question filters out the
freeloaders.
