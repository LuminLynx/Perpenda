# Tester outreach — PM list

Goal: recruit ~12 product managers as closed-test testers (Google Play needs
12 opted in for 14 continuous days). Quality over quantity — real PMs give
real feedback *and* satisfy the requirement.

## Ideal tester (tier these; work Tier 1 first)
- **Tier 1** — PMs/PMMs shipping AI/LLM features now (feel the pain firsthand)
- **Tier 2** — founders / product leads at early-stage AI startups
- **Tier 3** — APMs / PMs wanting to level up on AI
- Skip: pure engineers, non-product folks

## Where to find named PMs (public sources)
- Product Hunt makers/hunters of AI products
- Indie Hackers profiles
- PM/AI newsletter authors + engaged commenters
- PM podcast guests
- Community #intros channels (PM Slacks/Discords)
- AI startup team/about pages (PM titles)
- Product + AI meetup / conference speaker lists

## Process
1. Fill `tester-outreach.csv` to ~30 names (import into Google Sheets).
2. Personalize each first line (reference their work) — never a mass blast.
3. Ask for *feedback*, not "test my app." Offer early access + a say in the roadmap.
4. Track status: To contact → Reached out → Replied → In test → Declined.
5. One polite follow-up after ~4–5 days if no reply. Then drop it.

## Outreach message (personalize the first line per person)
> Hi [name] — saw you're working on [their AI/product thing]. I built a small
> app, Perpenda, that trains the AI-judgment calls PMs increasingly have to make
> (read a real LLM trade-off → make the call → get graded criterion by criterion).
> I'm looking for a few PMs to test it for ~2 weeks. Android, free, no ads/trackers.
> Early access + a real say in what ships. Open to it? I'll send the invite + steps.
